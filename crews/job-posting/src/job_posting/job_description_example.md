# Amazing Job Description Example
# ================================
# 职位描述示例文件（参考模板）
# ================================

# 本文件的作用：
# ──────────────
# 这是一个标准化的 JD（职位描述）模板示例。
# 撰稿人代理 (writer_agent) 会通过 FileReadTool 读取此文件，
# 作为格式和结构参考，确保生成的职位描述遵循最佳实践。

# 文件结构说明：
# ──────────────
# 一个完整的 JD 通常包含以下部分：
#
#   1. Company Overview     — 公司概述（吸引候选人的第一印象）
#   2. Job Title            — 职位名称
#   3. Location             — 工作地点
#   4. Job Summary          — 职位概述（一句话概括核心价值）
#   5. Responsibilities     — 工作职责（候选人日常要做什么）
#   6. Requirements         - 任职要求（硬性技能和经验门槛）
#   7. Benefits             — 福利待遇（吸引人才的亮点）
#   8. How to Apply         — 申请方式（行动号召）
#   9. Equal Opportunity    — 平等机会声明

## Company Overview
# 公司概述 —— 展示公司文化和价值主张
# 目的：让候选人了解这是否是一个他们想加入的公司
At InnovateTech, we're at the forefront of digital transformation, leveraging cutting-edge technologies to create impactful solutions. Our culture thrives on innovation, collaboration, and a commitment to excellence. Join us to be a part of a dynamic team shaping the future of tech.

## Job Title: Senior Software Engineer
# 职位名称 — 清晰明确，包含级别信息（Senior）

### Location
Remote - Global Team
# 工作地点 — 明确远程还是现场，以及地理位置

### Job Summary
# 职位概述 — 用 2-3 句话概括这个岗位的核心价值和吸引力
# 这是候选人最先关注的部分之一，需要简洁有力
As a Senior Software Engineer at InnovateTech, you'll lead the development of scalable software solutions that revolutionize how businesses interact with technology. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive projects from conception to deployment, ensuring high-quality and innovative outcomes.

### Responsibilities
# 工作职责 —— 具体列出该职位的日常工作内容
# 使用行为动词开头（设计、开发、领导、协作等）
- Design, develop, and implement high-quality software solutions that align with our strategic direction.
- Lead technical discussions and decision-making processes to drive technology forward.
- Mentor junior engineers, providing guidance and support to foster a culture of excellence and growth.
- Collaborate with stakeholders across the company to understand requirements and deliver beyond expectations.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies to incorporate best practices into our workflows.

### Requirements
# 任职要求 —— 候选人必须具备的条件（硬性门槛 + 软性素质）
# 分为：学历、经验、技术能力、软技能
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.                    # 学历要求
- 5+ years of experience in software development, with a strong background in [Specific Technology/Programming Language].  # 工作年限 + 核心技术
- Proven track record of leading successful projects from inception to completion.        # 项目经验
- Excellent problem-solving skills and a passion for technology.                          # 问题解决能力
- Strong communication and teamwork abilities.                                            # 软技能

### Benefits
# 福利待遇 —— 公司提供的员工福利，是吸引人才的重要因素
# 包括：薪酬、保险、休假、发展机会等
- Competitive salary and equity package.              # 有竞争力的薪酬和股权
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance. # 全面的健康/牙科/视力保险
- Unlimited PTO to promote work-life balance.         # 无限带薪休假
- Remote work flexibility.                            # 远程办公灵活性
- Professional development stipends.                 # 专业发展津贴
- Monthly wellness allowances.                       # 月度健康津贴
- Inclusive and dynamic work culture.                # 包容且充满活力的工作文化

### How to Apply
# 申请方式 —— 清晰的行动号召 (CTA)
# 告诉候选人如何申请：邮箱、所需材料等
Please submit your resume, cover letter, and any relevant portfolio links to careers@innovatetech.com with the subject "Senior Software Engineer Application". We're excited to hear from you!

# 平等机会声明 —— 法律合规 + 多元化承诺
InnovateTech is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
